# PRIVACY POLICY
## CKB - Code Knowledge Base
**Last Updated: March 2026**

### 1. WHO WE ARE
CKB ("we," "us," "our") provides architectural analysis for code. Our website: ckb.dev

### 2. DATA WE COLLECT

#### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Data**: Name, email, company, role
- **Billing Data**: Payment info (processed by Stripe)
- **Profile Data**: Preferences, settings
- **Content**: Your code for analysis
- **Communications**: Support requests, feedback

#### 2.2 Automatically Collected
- **Usage Data**: Features used, frequency, duration
- **Technical Data**: IP address, browser, device
- **Performance Data**: Load times, errors
- **Analytics**: Page views, clicks, referrals

#### 2.3 From Third Parties
- **AI Tools**: Through MCP integration
- **GitHub**: If you connect repositories
- **Payment Processors**: Confirmation of payments

### 3. HOW WE USE DATA

#### 3.1 Core Operations
- Provide architectural analysis
- Maintain and improve service
- Authenticate users
- Process payments
- Customer support

#### 3.2 Legal Compliance
- Fulfill contractual obligations
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal requirements
- Enforce our terms

#### 3.3 With Consent
- Marketing communications (opt-out anytime)
- Product research participation
- Beta feature access

#### 3.4 Aggregated Data
- Anonymous usage statistics
- Benchmarking reports
- Industry trends
- **Never** individual code without permission

### 4. DATA SHARING

#### 4.1 Service Providers
- **Stripe**: Payment processing
- **AWS/Azure/GCP**: Cloud infrastructure
- **SendGrid/Resend**: Email delivery
- **Intercom**: Customer support
- **Datadog**: Monitoring

#### 4.2 Legal Requirements
- Court orders or legal process
- Protect rights and safety
